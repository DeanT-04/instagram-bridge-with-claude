# Code of Conduct

Heliograph is a small project, and we want it to be a good place to work.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and to people writing in their second (or third) language.
- Keep discussion on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision gracefully.
- Respect privacy. Don't share other people's accounts, handles, content or personal data in issues, PRs or fixtures.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised content and doxxing are not acceptable, in any project space or in private messages related to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour breaks this code.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile ([@DeanT-04](https://github.com/DeanT-04)). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
